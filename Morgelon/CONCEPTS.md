# MORGELON — Three New Horror Concepts

A refocus: not “another fungus zombie movie,” not cabin folklore, not found-footage possession.  
Three concepts that mainstream horror usually **blocks, soft-pedals, or never tries** — staged for **real Colorado mountain footage**.

**Body engine (all three ride on this):** real-coded Morgellons-like itch/strings/sores → exaggerated hallucinations (vein-worms, sleep-trees, spider-web shoots) → shot with **Dr. Pimple Popper “satisfying disgust”** extraction grammar. See [`EXTRACTION.md`](EXTRACTION.md).

Fiction only. Do not dig at real skin for footage — prosthetics only.

## Concept 1 — THE SINGLE TREE  
### Clonal landscape horror (Colorado aspens)

**Logline:** The aspen stand isn’t a forest. It’s one organism with a thousand trunks. Trails are capillaries. Hikers don’t get “infected” — they get **grafted**.

**What it avoids:** Person-as-mushroom. Jump-scare wildlife. Witch-in-the-woods folklore.

**What’s new:** Botanical clone consciousness as the antagonist. The mountain grove thinks in root pressure and gold-leaf seasons. Threads/filaments are root grafts seeking skin. When one trunk is cut, the grove *remembers the cut in you*.

**Colorado truth to steal (fictionally amplified):** Real clonal aspen colonies (Pando-class idea) — one genome, many stems. Autumn gold is a single body changing clothes.

**Game / film beat:** Map = root map. “Safe rooms” are granite outcrops the clone can’t graft through. Boss = standing inside the oldest clone core at dusk.

**Image:** Drone over a golden aspen sea that breathes once, together.

## Concept 2 — HIGH CONSENSUS  
### Altitude synchronization horror

**Logline:** Above the treeline, thin air doesn’t make the party hallucinate. It makes them **agree**. The clearer they feel, the less anyone can dissent. The mountain edits the group into one mind.

**What it avoids:** “It was hypoxia / unreliable narrator” twist. Cabin fever arguments. Possession voice acting.

**What’s new:** Infection = **social synchronization under elevation**. Radios start overlapping. GPS tracks converge. Journal entries become identical handwriting. The horror is that everyone is calm, kind, and finished arguing.

**Colorado stage:** Independence Pass, high 14er approaches, Guanella / Loveland-area altitudes (legal, safe day hikes only — fiction does the rest). Night cold + tourist overlooks.

**Game / film beat:** Bloom meter rises with elevation gain. Choices disappear from dialogue trees as Consensus climbs. Ending fork: descend alone (survive, disbelieved) or summit together (Joined).

**Image:** Three hikers turn toward camera on the same frame at a windy overlook — smiling.

## Concept 3 — LEAF-PEEPING ESCHATON  
### Tourist gaze / drone ritual horror

**Logline:** The Chorus only finishes fruiting during Colorado’s fall color. Leaf-peepers and DJI pilots don’t document the apocalypse — **their looking completes it**. Gold is bait. Cameras are stamens.

**What it avoids:** Haunted VHS. “We found this footage.” Creepypasta camcorder. Anti-tech Luddite sermon.

**What’s new:** High-production tourism as fertility rite. Scenic overlooks are temples. The more beautiful the shot, the deeper the graft. Your DJI master shots are diegetic — the movie *is* the ritual.

**Colorado stage:** Maroon Bells / Aspen-area color (permits!), Kebler Pass, Castle Creek, San Juans gold belts, Twin Lakes overlooks — shoot what’s legal and safe; fiction names can mask exact peaks.

**Game / film beat:** Inventory includes memory cards. Deleting a clip lowers Bloom but erases Evidence. Keeping 4K gold footage unlocks the true ending *and* grafts the player.

**Image:** Parking-lot tripods at golden hour; filaments in the viewfinders only.

## How they braid into one movie/game

Working title still **MORGELON** — but the engine is:

1. **Single Tree** = ecology / map / body graft (Fly macros of root-silk)  
2. **High Consensus** = social / systems / dialogue collapse (RE pressure without zombies)  
3. **Leaf-Peeping Eschaton** = camera / meta / your real DJI unit  

Facility/clinic remains a **valley-floor airlock** (medevac, visitor center, ranger station) — denial before the switchbacks.

### Tone rules for the new sort of movie
- Beauty first, dread second — Colorado must look worth dying for  
- No comic mushrooms; no purple alien goo  
- Consensus feels loving until it isn’t  
- Never punish the audience for wanting a pretty drone shot — implicate them

### One-sentence pitch (poster)
**In the Colorado gold, the forest is one body, the altitude agrees for you, and every perfect drone shot finishes the harvest.**
