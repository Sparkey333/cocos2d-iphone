# MORGELON — Game Design Bible

Fiction horror. Not medical advice.

## Pitch

A survival-horror facility crawl (Resident Evil bones) where the enemy is a **fungal group mind** rewriting flesh — shot and graded with the wet, practical, tragic body-horror production value of **The Fly** (Cronenberg), not cartoon zombies.

Threads / filaments are the visible mycelium. Aspen-white structures are fruiting bodies in meat. Denial is the social armor of the outbreak.

## Three pillars

### 1. Resident Evil structure
- Locked facility / clinic / quarantine wing / sub-basement “greenhouse”
- Key items, door puzzles, scarce ammo/meds, map tension
- Safe rooms with typewriter-or-equivalent save ritual (cassette dictaphone + spore swab)
- Boss gates = fruiting chambers; trash mobs = “Joined” — people mid-overwrite

### 2. Fungal evil group mind
- **The Chorus**: a mycelial intelligence that thinks in many mouths
- Infection is consensus — the more hosts, the smarter/faster it speaks
- Filaments carry memory; pull a thread and you hear someone else’s last minute
- Environmental tells: fruiting on vents, cordyceps-like posture, aspen trunks punching through tile

### 3. The Fly look (production value)
- Close-ups of transformation that feel **practical**: sweat, resin, hair, chitin-sheen filaments
- Tragedy first — the host is still a person while the rewrite happens
- Wet labs, plastic sheeting, amber practicals, telepod-adjacent medical tech (teleporters optional; **telefusion anxiety** mandatory)
- Grade: cold steel + sick fluorescents; never comic-book gore splash

## Player fantasy

You are the one who still flinches. Everyone else is syncing to the Chorus. Your inventory is proof. Your map is a crime scene. Your body is becoming evidence.

## Loop

Explore → find key/tool → survive Joined encounter → optional thread-pull (lore + bloom risk) → unlock deeper zone → fruiting boss → temporary silence → louder Chorus.

## Systems (v1)

| System | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Bloom** | 0–100. Raises from thread-pulls, spore rooms, Chorus proximity. High bloom = audio hallucinations + filament VFX on HUD |
| **Chorus Sync** | Enemy coordination. Break line-of-sight / burn fruiting nodes to desync packs |
| **Evidence** | Collectibles that contradict clinic lies; unlock endings |
| **Inventory** | RE-tight grid: antifungals, burn gel, latch keys, spore mask filters, cassette saves |
| **Joined AI** | Idle sway (fruiting), then sudden many-angle approach when Chorus pulses |

## Zones

1. **Intake Clinic** — denial, fluorescents, paperwork as puzzle
2. **Quarantine Flood** — plastic sheeting, knee water, lies on loudspeakers
3. **Mycelial Stack** — vertical shafts of filament; The Fly lab energy
4. **Aspen Vein** — fruiting cathedral; group mind speaks in unison
5. **Cold Room** — optional true ending if Evidence ≥ threshold and Bloom < 60

## Tone rules

- Horror of recognition, not jump-scare spam
- The Chorus is persuasive; doctors are wrong *and* terrified
- No purple neon hive; no comedy mushrooms
- If a shot could pass as a lost reel from a 1980s body-horror feature, keep it
