# MORGELON — Extraction Horror (satisfying disgust)

Fiction film grammar. **Not medical advice.**  
If you have real fibers, sores, or itching: see a dermatologist; do **not** dig at your skin for footage. On set we use **prosthetics, gel, thread, silicone ulcers only**.

---

## The escalation ladder (story spine)

Ground the first act in sensations people actually report with Morgellons-like complaints — then **lie bigger** on purpose as Bloom rises.

| Stage | What the audience gets | What’s “real-coded” vs exaggerated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0 · Itch** | Always itching. Can’t finish a sentence without scratching. | Grounded |
| **1 · Strings** | Pale / colored threads at sores; ulcer-like spots | Grounded (reported) |
| **2 · Pull compulsion** | Camera becomes Dr. Pimple Popper: light, tweezers, the *get it out* hunger | Aesthetic (satisfying disgust) |
| **3 · Worse for looking** | Every extraction / every photo makes threads denser | Fiction rule |
| **4 · Hallucination graft** | “Maggots” / worms sliding in veins; spider-web shoots from pores in sleep; aspens cropping up under eyelids | Full exaggerate |
| **5 · Landscape rhyme** | Colorado clone stand = the dream forest made daylight | Concepts braid |

**Hard rule of the movie:** Documentation and extraction feel good for one second — then the map of filaments expands. Satisfaction is the bait. Relapse is the joke.

---

## Harness “Dr. Pimple Popper” without becoming a clinic YouTube

Steal the **grammar**, not the brand:

1. **Dead-center macro** — pore / sore / filament fills the frame  
2. **Clean light** — bright, almost beauty-light on something awful  
3. **Tool enter frame late** — tweezers, tape, needle-nose (prop)  
4. **The release sound** — wet tick, thread whisper (Foley gold)  
5. **Hold after the pull** — longer than comfort; show the hole wanting more  
6. **No jokey host voice** — silence, breath, itch ASMR; horror owns the aftertaste  

Avoid: laughing commentary, before/after thumbs-up, cartoon pus fountain.  
Keep: the shameful urge to watch, the relief, the immediate “there’s another one.”

**Tone name:** *satisfying disgust* — audience leans in, then regrets leaning in, then leans in again.

## Visual menu (practical → hallucinated)

### Practical (shootable with DJI Pocket / phone macro)
- Silicone ulcer with buried embroidery thread  
- Gel + chartreuse filament “root-silk”  
- Ulcer edge that weeps glycerin (not gore carnival)  
- Itch: nails just above skin, never breaking real skin  

### Hallucinated inserts (VFX / dream / Higgsfield)
- Vein-tunnel worms (pearl / translucent, not creature-feature crabs)  
- Sleep: filaments **crop up like aspen saplings** through bedsheets / eyelids  
- Wake: threads **shoot out like spider webs** across a dark cabin ceiling  
- Mirror: web from mouth corners to ears — gone when lights slam on  

### Colorado rhyme
Dream trees = daylight clonal stand. Web shoots = drone canopy grid. Worms in blood = switchback rivers in the valley.

## Sound design

- Continuous soft **itch bed** (fabric, nail, dry skin Foley) under whole film  
- Extraction: close mic wet *tick* + exhaled relief  
- After pull: high thin **whine** that means Bloom went up  
- Sleep web: silk zip + aspen leaf-quake crossfade

## Game / interactive hook

| Action | Feel | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Extract** | Popper satisfaction, Evidence +1 | Bloom +7, new filament spawns |
| **Photograph** | Leaf-peeping / proof | Bloom +3, hallucination chance up |
| **Ignore itch** | Consensus pressure / social calm | Sanity ok, miss Evidence |
| **Sleep** | Dream forest / web shoot cutscene | Mandatory hallucination beat |

Win condition isn’t “clean skin.” It’s **stop extracting** while still holding enough Evidence to be believed — almost impossible. That’s the horror.

---

## Director mantra

> Show the pull they came for. Punish them with more thread.  
> Make the itch louder than the jump scare.  
> Gross enough to satisfy. Beautiful enough to be Colorado.
