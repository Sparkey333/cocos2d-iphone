Top three concepts mainstream horror usually blocks, soft-pedals, or exhausts — rebuilt for real high-country footage.

01

The Single Tree

Clonal landscape horror

Avoids: mushroom zombies, witch-cabin folklore.

The aspen stand is one organism with a thousand trunks. Hikers aren’t infected — they’re grafted. Trails are capillaries. Cut one stem; the grove remembers it in you.

02

High Consensus

Altitude synchronization horror

Avoids: “it was hypoxia / unreliable narrator.”

Thin air doesn’t confuse the party — it makes them agree. Calm smiles. Identical journals. Dialogue choices vanish as elevation rises.

03

Leaf-Peeping Eschaton

Tourist gaze / drone ritual

Avoids: haunted VHS found-footage.

Fall gold is bait. Tripods and DJI pilots don’t document the harvest — looking completes it. Beauty is the ritual. Your plates are diegetic.
